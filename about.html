Our Office

The Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (MDRRMO) of Los Baños, Laguna is committed to protecting lives, safeguarding properties, and building a safe, prepared, and disaster-resilient community. Through proactive disaster preparedness, effective emergency response, continuous capacity-building, and strong collaboration with barangays, government agencies, and community partners, the office works to minimize disaster risks and strengthen local resilience. Guided by the principles of prevention, preparedness, response, and recovery.

The MDRRMO continuously enhances its capabilities to address emergencies and emerging hazards. Above all, it upholds its commitment to serve with integrity, compassion, teamwork, and excellence—toward a safer and more resilient Los Baños.

Our Journey

Milestones

MDRRMO Milestones

2014

Establishment and Institutionalization of the MDRRMO

Formal establishment and organization of the Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (MDRRMO) of Los Baños.

Operationalization of the Municipal Emergency Operations Center (EOC).

Acquisition and deployment of the municipality's initial ambulance units to strengthen emergency medical response. Strengthening of the Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council (MDRRMC) and inter-agency coordination.

2015

Strengthening of Disaster Preparedness and Emergency Response

Continuation of municipal disaster preparedness initiatives and emergency response operations.

Enhancement of coordination among barangays, partner agencies, and emergency responders.

2016-2018

Continuous Improvement of Response Capabilities

Acquisition and upgrading of disaster response equipment and operational resources. Enhancement of emergency response, rescue, and preparedness capabilities. Continuing implementation of community awareness, disaster preparedness, and capacity-building activities.

2019

Establishment of the MDRRMO Operations Center and Institutionalization of Rescue Volunteers

Establishment of the MDRRMO Operations Center to strengthen emergency monitoring, coordination, and response management.

Institutionalization of the MDRRM Rescue Volunteers through Municipal Ordinance No. 2019-1890, providing a formal framework for volunteer participation, training, and emergency response support.

2020–2021

Sustaining Emergency Response and Public Safety Operations

Continued delivery of emergency response and public safety services amid evolving community needs and emergencies. Strengthening of coordination, preparedness, and operational readiness among responders

2022

Recognition for Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Excellence

Recognition of the Municipal Government of Los Baños under the 22nd Gawad KALASAG Seal for Excellence in Disaster Risk Reduction and Management and Humanitarian Assistance, receiving Fully Compliant LGU status.

2023

Sustained Institutional Development and Community Preparedness

Continued strengthening of disaster preparedness, emergency response, and community-based disaster risk reduction initiatives.Enhancement of responder competencies and coordination with barangay DRRM teams and partner organizations.

2024

Enhancement of Rescue and Emergency Response Capabilities

Acquisition of additional rescue vehicles to improve emergency mobility, operational efficiency, and response capabilities.

Continued acquisition and upgrading of disaster response equipment and resources.

Further strengthening of rescue operations and emergency response readiness.

2025

Strengthening Disaster Preparedness, Response, and Monitoring Systems

Continued implementation of disaster preparedness programs, emergency response operations, community-based DRRM activities, and responder capability-building.

Further enhancement of the MDRRMO Operations Center through initiatives involving CCTV monitoring, automated weather monitoring, response coordination, and public safety and security monitoring.

Strengthening of partnerships with barangays, government agencies, and other stakeholders.

2026

Advancing Integrated Monitoring, Risk Assessment, and Disaster Resilience

Further development of integrated CCTV monitoring, automated weather monitoring, emergency response, and public safety systems through intergovernmental collaboration.

On September 2, 2026, the Municipal Government's partnership with the Provincial Government of Laguna was formalized through a Memorandum of Agreement to strengthen monitoring and emergency coordination.

Strengthening of disaster risk assessment, contingency planning, and preparation of the 2027 Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Fund.

Continuing enhancement of the municipality's disaster preparedness and response capabilities.
