Frontline Services

Emergency, First Aid, Fire & Rescue

🚑

Emergency Response

24/7 response to medical emergencies, accidents and disasters across the municipality.

  • Ambulance and patient transport
  • Incident command and coordination
  • Emergency operations center monitoring
⛑️

First Aid & Basic Life Support

Hands-on training and on-site medical assistance during events and emergencies.

  • First aid and CPR/AED training
  • Medical standby for community events
  • Emergency medical technician support
🔥

Fire Safety & Support

Promoting fire prevention and supporting fire suppression operations.

  • Fire safety seminars and drills
  • Support to fire responders
  • Household fire-prevention tips
🛟

Search & Rescue

Trained rescuers for flood, landslide, vehicular and other technical rescue situations.

  • Water and flood rescue
  • Vehicular extrication
  • Search, retrieval and evacuation
