Be Prepared

Safety Tips

Know what to do before, during and after typhoons, earthquakes, fires and floods. Preparedness saves lives.

What To Do

Choose a Hazard

Select a disaster below to see the steps to take before, during and after.

🛡️ BEFORE
  • Monitor PAGASA and MDRRMO advisories regularly.
  • Know your barangay's evacuation center and the safest route to it.
  • Prepare your go-bag, charge phones and power banks.
  • Secure loose roofing, windows and outdoor objects.
  • Store clean drinking water and ready-to-eat food.
⚠️ DURING
  • Stay indoors, away from windows and glass.
  • Evacuate immediately if ordered by authorities.
  • Unplug appliances and avoid using electricity if flooding starts.
  • Keep a radio or phone on for official updates.
  • Do not cross flooded roads, rivers or bridges.
✅ AFTER
  • Wait for the official "all clear" before going home.
  • Stay away from fallen or sagging power lines and report them.
  • Check your home for damage before turning on the power.
  • Boil or treat drinking water if the supply may be contaminated.
  • Report damage and people in need to your barangay or MDRRMO.
🛡️ BEFORE
  • Identify safe spots: under sturdy tables, away from glass and heavy objects.
  • Secure cabinets, shelves and appliances to walls.
  • Know how to shut off electricity, water and gas.
  • Agree on a family meeting point outside the house.
  • Join earthquake drills in your school, office or barangay.
⚠️ DURING
  • DROP to your hands and knees.
  • COVER your head and neck under a sturdy table, or beside an interior wall.
  • HOLD ON until the shaking stops.
  • Stay away from windows, mirrors and falling objects.
  • If outdoors, move to an open area away from buildings, posts and trees.
  • If driving, pull over in a clear area and stay inside.
✅ AFTER
  • Check yourself and others for injuries; give first aid if trained.
  • Expect aftershocks: drop, cover and hold on again.
  • Evacuate if the building is cracked or damaged.
  • If you smell gas, open windows, avoid flames and leave.
  • Use text messages instead of calls to keep phone lines open.
⚠️
Do not run outside during shaking and do not use elevators. Most injuries happen from falling objects while moving.
🛡️ BEFORE
  • Have wiring and electrical connections checked regularly.
  • Never overload outlets or use damaged cords.
  • Keep matches, lighters and candles away from children.
  • Install smoke alarms and keep a fire extinguisher at home.
  • Plan two exit routes and keep them clear.
⚠️ DURING
  • Shout "Sunog!" to alert everyone, then get out.
  • Stay low under the smoke and cover your nose and mouth with a cloth.
  • Feel doors before opening; if hot, use another exit.
  • Never go back inside for belongings.
  • Call 911 or the Bureau of Fire Protection once you are safe.
  • If clothes catch fire: Stop, Drop and Roll.
✅ AFTER
  • Stay out until firefighters say the building is safe.
  • Seek medical attention for burns or smoke inhalation.
  • Have the electrical and gas systems inspected before use.
  • Report the incident to your barangay and the BFP.
🍳
Never leave cooking unattended and never use water on a grease or electrical fire. Cover the pan or use an appropriate extinguisher.
🛡️ BEFORE
  • Know if your area is flood- or landslide-prone.
  • Keep important documents in a waterproof bag.
  • Clear canals and drainage around your home.
  • Prepare to evacuate early when heavy rain continues.
  • Watch for warning signs of landslides: new cracks, tilting trees or posts, muddy water in streams.
⚠️ DURING
  • Move to higher ground immediately.
  • Turn off the main power switch if water is entering the house.
  • Avoid walking or driving through floodwater; even shallow, fast water can sweep you away.
  • Keep away from rivers, creeks and steep slopes.
  • Call 911 or your barangay if you need rescue.
✅ AFTER
  • Return home only when authorities say it is safe.
  • Floodwater may carry disease: wash hands and clean wounds right away.
  • Throw away food that touched floodwater.
  • Watch for leptospirosis symptoms (fever, body pain, red eyes) after wading in floodwater and see a doctor.
  • Have wiring checked before switching the power back on.

Quick Reminders

Do's and Don'ts in Any Emergency

✅ Do

  • Stay calm and think before acting.
  • Follow instructions from MDRRMO, barangay officials and responders.
  • Keep emergency numbers saved and written down.
  • Check on neighbors, older adults, children and persons with disabilities.
  • Share only verified information from official sources.

🚫 Don't

  • Don't ignore evacuation orders.
  • Don't spread rumors or unverified posts.
  • Don't touch fallen power lines or wet electrical equipment.
  • Don't go sightseeing at disaster areas; it blocks responders.
  • Don't call the hotline for non-urgent concerns.
