About MDRRMO

Resilient communities start with preparedness

The Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office leads planning, coordination and implementation of disaster risk reduction programs in Los Baños, Laguna.

From early warning to recovery, we partner with barangays, schools, agencies and volunteers so that no one is left behind when disaster strikes.
